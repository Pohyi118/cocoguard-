# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Cocoa scouts, smallholder growers and agricultural advisers in Malaysia (first pilot: a farm in Tanjung Sepat, Selangor). The grower or adviser plans on a laptop or phone at the farm office; the scout walks the route outdoors on a phone, often in bright sun or drizzle, with wet or muddy hands, after heavy rain. GSC competition judges see the same screens as evidence that this is a real, usable tool.

## Product Purpose

KokoShield Field Desk turns a rain event into a short, ranked scouting route: which blocks to walk first within a fixed time budget, why, and what was found. Screens: Field plan (route after rain), Walk (scout's phone during the round), Scout log (review and confirm cases), Compare (priority route vs the grower's usual round), Sensor node (optional local readings). Success: a scout finds more confirmed black pod per scouting hour, and the grower trusts the plan enough to follow it.

## Brand Personality

Grounded, precise, calm. Like a field instrument or a good map app: quiet confidence, data you can trust, nothing decorative. Plain, specific language in English and Bahasa Malaysia.

## Anti-references

- Generic SaaS dashboard: white card grids, KPI tiles, pill toggles everywhere, identical rounded buttons.
- Glassy AI-startup look: frosted glass cards, soft wide shadows, gradients, everything floating.

## Design Principles

1. The farm is the interface: the map carries the information; UI chrome stays out of its way.
2. One clear next action per screen (Start scouting, Found / Nothing found, Confirm).
3. Show the reason, not just the rank: every priority has its cause visible (slow drainage, prior record, rain over trigger).
4. Honest about demo data, once, plainly; never repeated caveats.
5. Built for the field: legible in sunlight, large targets, works offline.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG 2.2 AA minimum everywhere. Walk screen is outdoor-first: high contrast, body text 17px or more, touch targets 48px or more, no information carried by colour alone. Respect reduced motion. Bilingual EN / BM.
