KokoShield pitch video (v8): sources and credits
===============================================

Footage
- Farm, farmer, storm, drone and cocoa growth time-lapse footage: AI-generated with Google Flow (Omni 1.1 Flash). The farm and the farmer are fictional and illustrative. They are not the pilot site or a real grower.
- The farm footage starts from or lands on prototype/assets/farm_aerial.jpg, the illustrative aerial image the app uses.
- App footage: screen recordings of the KokoShield Field Desk prototype (prototype/KokoShield_Prototype.html) with its demo data.
- Field Node: rendered from the team's CAD model (KokoShield_Field_Node.glb, prototype kit). Concept prototype.

Figures
- 20-30% of global cocoa production lost to black pod each year, spread by rain splash: Guest, D. (2007), as cited in the GSC proposal.
- 86% of Malaysia's cultivated cocoa area is smallholdings (5,662 of 6,580 ha, 2025): Malaysian Cocoa Board, as cited in the GSC proposal.
- 48 mm rain, 30 mm alarm level, route A-D-B-G, 7 vs 2 pods and 0 vs 2 missed blocks: the prototype's demo data, labelled on screen as demo data.
- First pilot at a cocoa farm in Tanjung Sepat, Selangor (in talks), on a RM1,200 budget: GSC proposal and team.
- Pilot plan (4 post-rain rounds over about 6 weeks; adviser reviews, travel, supplies, contingency) and SDG targets 2.3, 2.4 and 13.1: GSC proposal v3.
