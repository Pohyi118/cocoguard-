Honesty notes

Photos show cocoa farming in Vietnam, Indonesia, the Solomon Islands, Ghana, Sri Lanka, Ecuador and a rice paddy in India; none were taken on the pilot farm. The drone survey, route race and counts are a demo simulation on the app's demo farm (prototype preset findings). The farmer in the pending clips is fictional and AI-generated. The voice is AI-generated. The 20–30% figure is Guest (2007); the RM1,200 ask matches the proposal's pilot budget.
