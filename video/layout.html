Music arc

Scenes 1–3 sit on a low, tense B-minor pad with sparse marimba under the rain. A riser builds into scene 4, where the solution appears and the music lifts into a hopeful D-major theme with bass and a soft kick. A light shaker drives the scene 6 race, and the score resolves and fades on the closing shot. Music dips about 60% whenever someone speaks.

Finishing and reusing it

1. Optional: your own voices
  • The AI voice is ready to submit. Real team voices can still feel more personal to judges.
  • Record one take per scene, reading the script above. Each take must fit its “speech ≤” time.
  • Save the takes as video/audio/s1.wav … s8.wav.
2. Rebuild
  • In the video folder run python voice.py --recordings (or python voice.py for the AI voice).
  • Then python sound.py to remix music and effects under the new voice.
  • Then python capture3d.py (about 12 minutes) to render the MP4 again.
3. Interactive page
  • Private link: https://claude.ai/artifact/2tEKvFyTkykWYMHAH7ot2T. Share it from the page’s Share menu so others can open it.
  • Local copy: KokoShield_3D/index.html in Chrome or Edge (internet needed for the 3D library).
  • Good for the Build-phase live demo: Play the story, Explore 3D to click blocks, or Your turn to plan a route.

Honesty notes

The farm, rainfall, pod counts, route race and challenge results are a demo simulation based on the prototype’s preset findings (7 vs 2), labelled on screen. Field results will come from the pilot. The Field Node is shown as a CAD concept. The voice is AI-generated.
