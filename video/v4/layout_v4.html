Climate risk
Real black pod rot close-ups with the 20–30% count-up, drying beans in Ghana with three country chips, and an Indonesian smallholder pruning cocoa with the 86% donut.Fading rain, tick runs on the count-ups, pops on cards and chips. “Black pod spreads by rain splash and costs an estimated twenty to thirty percent of global cocoa production. Heavy rain is linked to lower yields in Ghana, Ecuador and Indonesia. In Malaysia, eighty-six percent of cocoa land is smallholdings, and advisers can't visit every farm after every storm.”

Pilot
Route race on the app’s map: ranked and usual scouts walk at once against a 30-minute clock; finds pop (+3, +2, +1, +1); “Missed by usual round” flags; then the pilot facts.Start beeps, clock ticks, bright dings for ranked finds and duller ones for the usual round, finish bell, two low buzzes for the misses. Shaker joins the beat. “In our pilot, two scouts walk the same thirty minutes on one farm: one on the ranked route, one on the usual round. A follow-up check counts what each missed. We go ahead only if the ranked route finds more confirmed pods per hour in three of four rounds.”

Finishing and reusing it

1. Optional: your own voices
  • Record one take per scene reading the script above. Save as video/v4/audio/s1.wav … s9.wav.
  • Run python voice_v4.py --recordings. Scene lengths follow your takes; keep the total under 2:00.
2. Rebuild
  • python make_sound_v4.py then python sound_v4.py remixes music and effects under the voice.
  • python capture_v4.py (about 8 minutes) renders the MP4 again.
3. Interactive page
  • The 3D story page stays a separate demo: https://claude.ai/artifact/2tEKvFyTkykWYMHAH7ot2T (private until shared).
  • It does not yet include the drone; ask to add it if you will demo it live.

Honesty notes

The photos show cocoa farming in other countries and were not taken on the pilot farm. The drone photo shows another operator’s flight, labelled as illustrative. The drone survey, route race and counts are a concept and demo simulation on the app’s demo farm. The Field Node is a CAD concept. The voice is AI-generated.
