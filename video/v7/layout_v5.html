Honesty notes

Footage comes from Pexels (Pexels License); photos show cocoa farming in Vietnam, Indonesia, the Solomon Islands, Ghana, Sri Lanka, Ecuador and a rice paddy in India; none were taken on the pilot farm. The drone survey, route race and counts are a demo simulation on the prototype's demo farm (preset findings); the app footage is the real prototype running on demo data, and its aerial view is illustrative. The two scouts are stock footage; their routes and finds are a demo simulation on the prototype map. The voice is AI-generated. The 20–30% figure is Guest (2007); the RM1,200 ask matches the proposal's pilot budget.
