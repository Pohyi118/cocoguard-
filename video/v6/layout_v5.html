The route
Drone hologram marks standing water in A, D and G and the inputs check in. Then real prototype footage: the cursor checks blocks on the aerial map, switches to the usual round (B, C, E, G) and back to Priority (A, D, B, G), reads the route list and clicks Start scouting.Drone hum, water drips, four input pops, a rising sweep as the route draws, a chime. Honesty notes

Photos show cocoa farming in Vietnam, Indonesia, the Solomon Islands, Ghana, Sri Lanka, Ecuador and a rice paddy in India; none were taken on the pilot farm. The drone survey, route race and counts are a demo simulation on the prototype's demo farm (preset findings); the app footage is the real prototype running on demo data, and its aerial view is illustrative. The farmer in the pending clips is fictional and AI-generated. The voice is AI-generated. The 20–30% figure is Guest (2007); the RM1,200 ask matches the proposal's pilot budget.
